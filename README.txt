GOLU MATHS — 50 QUESTIONS / FIVE PER SESSION
Extract ZIP and open index.html. Keep style.css and script.js alongside it.

50 unique problems: 14 addition, 14 subtraction, 11 multiplication, 11 division.
Each session shuffles five unique questions: one of each operation plus one extra.
Questions from the immediately previous session are excluded. Refresh resets history.
Answers are whole numbers from 0 to 10. Division has no remainders.
Bounce once per unit, then Check answer. For zero, check without bouncing.
Use -1, Clear, and Show me how for support. No timers or penalties.
After five correct answers, a trophy and confetti celebrate completion.
Play 5 new questions starts a fresh shuffled session. New shuffled session restarts anytime.
Reduced-motion settings replace animation with a static celebration.
English voice depends on device support. No installation or account needed.
Multiplication/division are grown-up-supported extensions for young children.
Logic tested across 100 sessions (500 answers); visual browser testing unavailable.
